03 — Purchases, billing and renewals

Purchases, billing and renewals

All purchases of Culmo Pro and of Alarm Clock: Heavy Sleepers Premium are processed by Google Play. Culmo uses RevenueCat as billing infrastructure; Alarm Clock: Heavy Sleepers uses Google Play Billing directly. RG App Studio never sees, handles or stores your card, UPI or other payment details — they go to Google Play, not to us.

That means your payment relationship for the transaction itself is with Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service, and any payment-method, tax or invoicing questions are handled there. For Culmo, what we receive from RevenueCat is an anonymous app-user identifier and whether your subscription is active — never your workout logs, body measurements, name or photo. Alarm Clock: Heavy Sleepers receives only Google Play's confirmation that your purchase is active, never your alarms or wake history. This is set out in full in our Privacy Policy.

How the subscriptions renew

  • The monthly and yearly plans are auto-renewing subscriptions. Google Play charges your payment method at the start of each period and renews automatically until you cancel.
  • The renewal amount is the price for your plan in your country at the time of renewal. Google Play may place an authorisation hold on your payment method shortly before a renewal — in India this can be up to five days ahead of the renewal date.
  • The lifetime unlock is a one-time purchase. It never renews and is never billed again. It applies to the app you bought it in, on the Google account you bought it with.

Delivery

Both apps are digital products delivered entirely through Google Play. Nothing is shipped physically, and there is no delivery address or delivery charge. Pro and Premium features unlock on your device as soon as Google Play confirms the purchase — usually within seconds. If a purchase went through but nothing has unlocked, open the app's Settings and use Restore purchases (in Alarm Clock: Heavy Sleepers, tap Go Premium first); if that does not fix it, email us and we will help.

04 — Cancellations

Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel a Culmo Pro or Alarm Clock: Heavy Sleepers Premium subscription at any time, and you cancel it in Google Play rather than inside the app — because Google Play, not us, holds the subscription.

On your Android phone

  1. Open the Play Store app and tap your profile icon.
  2. Tap Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select the app (Culmo or Alarm Clock: Heavy Sleepers), tap Cancel subscription, and follow the instructions.

On a computer

  1. Go to play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
  2. Click Manage next to the app (Culmo or Alarm Clock: Heavy Sleepers), then Cancel subscription.
  3. Select a reason and click Continue.

What happens after you cancel

Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not end your access straight away: Pro or Premium stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. If you cancel on day 3 of a monthly plan, you keep Pro until day 30; if you cancel six months into a yearly plan, you keep Pro until that year ends.

We charge no cancellation fee, and cancelling is a normal, expected thing to do — there is no retention maze, no "are you sure" gauntlet, and nothing you have to email us to unlock. The current period is not automatically refunded pro rata when you cancel; if you think it should be refunded, see the refund policy below and just ask us.

The one-time lifetime purchase is not a subscription. It never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

When Pro ends, Culmo returns to being the free logger. Your workout history is not deleted — it is on your device and stays there, and you can still log, view and export it. When Premium ends, Alarm Clock: Heavy Sleepers stays a complete free alarm clock: your alarms are not deleted, and alarms you have already saved keep working.

If you paid through UPI AutoPay, note that a cancelled subscription may keep showing as active in your payments centre until the current billing period ends. That is Google Play's display behaviour, not a second charge.

Within 48 hours: request it yourself

Google Play's own refund window is 48 hours from purchase, and it covers subscriptions and the one-time lifetime purchase alike:

  1. Go to play.google.com and click your profile picture.
  2. Click Payments & subscriptions, then Budget & order history.
  3. Find the order (Culmo or Alarm Clock: Heavy Sleepers) and click Report a problem.
  4. Select the option that describes your situation, complete the form and note that you would like a refund, then click Submit.

You do not need our permission for this, and you do not need to tell us first.

After 48 hours: write to us

After 48 hours Google Play routes refund requests to the developer, so the decision comes to us. Email manangamestudio@gmail.com with your Google Play order number and a line about what went wrong. We usually reply within two business days, and we will issue a refund through Google Play wherever the request is reasonable — for example:

  • a purchase you did not intend to make, or one made on your account by someone else in your household;
  • a duplicate charge, or being charged twice for the same thing;
  • a renewal you did not realise was coming;
  • a Pro or Premium feature that does not work on your device and that we cannot fix for you.

What we can and cannot promise

We want to be straight about the limits. Refunds are issued through Google Play and the money is returned by Google, not paid out by us, so we cannot control how quickly it reaches you — but the decision on a post-48-hour request is ours to make, and we make it. Refunds are returned to the original payment method. Google also limits repeat refunds of the same product.

Timeline. We usually review a refund request within two business days of receiving it. If a purchase is refunded, the Pro or Premium access it paid for ends — a refunded lifetime unlock stops being a lifetime unlock. Either way, your workout history and your alarms are untouched: they live on your device and are never held hostage to a billing decision.

Renewals already taken for periods you actually used are not refunded automatically. But ask us anyway if something went wrong. We are one person and we read the emails; we would rather refund you than keep money you feel you did not get value from.

07 — Health & fitness disclaimer

Health & fitness disclaimer

Culmo is a fitness logging and training tool. It is not a medical device, and nothing in it is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Culmo's coaching features — the Adaptive Coach, Programs, the Program Generator, RPE autoregulation, warm-up suggestions, one-rep-max estimates and insights — are calculations based on the numbers you type in. They are informed suggestions, not instructions from a doctor or a physiotherapist, and they cannot see your form, your joints, your sleep, your medication or your history. An estimated one-rep max is an estimate; a suggested load is a suggestion.

Please talk to a qualified professional — a doctor, physiotherapist or a coach who can watch you lift — before starting a new training programme, changing one substantially, or returning to training after injury, illness, surgery or pregnancy, and especially if you have any cardiovascular, respiratory, metabolic or musculoskeletal condition.

Strength training carries real risk of injury. You lift at your own risk and remain responsible for your own decisions in the gym. Use your judgement, respect what your body tells you on the day, and stop if something hurts — a number on a screen is never a reason to keep going. If you feel chest pain, faintness, severe shortness of breath or any other worrying symptom during exercise, stop and seek medical help immediately.

Culmo does not diagnose anything, does not detect medical conditions, and should never be used in place of professional medical care.

Alarm Clock: Heavy Sleepers includes missions that ask you to move: squats, dancing, shaking the phone, carrying it around a room and similar. They are short wake-up tasks, not exercise programmes, and you do them at your own risk and within your own limits. Go at your own pace, stop if anything hurts, keep clear of anything you could trip on or knock over, and choose a non-physical mission if movement is not right for you. If you cannot finish a mission, the app offers a backup task instead.
